## Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies

This project used the AI assistant Claude, developed by Anthropic, during the development of the analysis code.
Its assistance included generating initial code for the analyses and figures, and improving the code documentation. All AI-generated content was reviewed, tested, and validated by human developers.
Additionally, AI tools were used to critique drafts of this article and to identify opportunities to better communicate with a broader audience.
The authors reviewed, tested, and validated all AI-generated content and take full responsibility for the content of this preprint.
